Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

Our support team typically responds to inquiries within 24 hours during business days. Community forum members may respond faster.

Is GraniteOS really free?

Yes! GraniteOS is completely free and open source. There are no hidden costs, licensing fees, or premium versions.

Can I use GraniteOS commercially?

Yes, you can use GraniteOS for commercial purposes. Check our license agreement for specific terms and conditions.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Please send security concerns to security@graniteos.com instead of our general support email. We take security seriously and will investigate promptly.

Where can I find documentation?

Comprehensive documentation is available on our wiki at wiki.graniteos.com

How can I contribute to GraniteOS?

We welcome contributions! Check out our GitHub repository for contribution guidelines. You can contribute code, documentation, translations, or help with community support.
