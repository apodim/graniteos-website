Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

Privacy First: GraniteOS is committed to your privacy. We do not collect any user data when you use the simulator. The only personal information we collect is when you voluntarily contact us.

1. What Data We Collect

In the Simulator

We collect zero data while you use GraniteOS Simulator. The simulator runs entirely on your computer and does not:

  • Track your usage or interactions
  • Monitor what applications you open
  • Record your files or activities
  • Send telemetry or analytics
  • Collect system information
  • Store any personal data locally or remotely

On Our Website

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics on our website. No browsing data is collected. Note: We use Netlify forms for the contact handling, which Netlify also sends us your IPv6 address. We will use it only for blocking in case of function abuse.

Contact Information Only

The only personal data we collect is when you voluntarily contact us through our contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message content

This information is collected only if and when you choose to contact us.

2. How We Use Contact Information

If you contact us through our contact form, we use your information only for:

  • Responding to your inquiry or message
  • Providing support or assistance

We do not use your contact information for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose without your explicit consent.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your email and contact information (if provided) are kept strictly confidential.

We may only disclose information if required by law.

4. Security

The GraniteOS Simulator is completely offline. Your experience stays on your computer with no data transmitted to our servers. This means:

  • Your simulator data is never sent anywhere
  • Your files remain completely private
  • You have full control of your computer

If you contact us via email, that communication uses standard email encryption and security protocols.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any tracking technologies. Our website does not track your behavior or preferences.

6. Your Control

Since we don't collect data from the simulator, there's nothing to opt out of. You have complete control:

  • Use the simulator without any data being collected
  • Contact us or not - it's entirely your choice
  • Delete your email history by contacting us

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access

You can request to see what contact information we have about you.

Deletion

You can request deletion of your contact information at any time, and we will remove it from our records.

To Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at apodimapps@gmail.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Since GraniteOS Simulator does not collect any data, there is no special concern for children's privacy in the simulator itself. Parents and guardians can allow children to use the simulator freely with complete privacy.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. Significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us:

Email: apodimapps@gmail.com
